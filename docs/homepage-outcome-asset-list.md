## Asset Request Checklist (Per Outcome)

For each selected outcome, gather:

- 1 primary image candidate
- 1 alternate image candidate
- 1 support artifact (diagram / workflow / system evidence)
- 1 short note:
  - what the image proves
  - what should be cropped/emphasized
  - any confidentiality constraints

## Art Direction Rules (Homepage Rail)

- Keep all images intentionally cropped (no raw full-screen dumps)
- Maintain consistent visual treatment:
  - ratio family
  - tonal grading
  - edge treatment
- Do not force all work to look equally "beautiful"
- Do make every asset feel deliberately selected and composed
