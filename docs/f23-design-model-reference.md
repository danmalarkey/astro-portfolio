## Structural distinctions

The source model separates several layers that should remain distinct in portfolio messaging.

### Design approach

How the work is understood and carried forward:

- Start with the business
- Discover opportunities
- Understand the people
- Activities before screens
- Concepts before features
- Understand the work
- Systems over screens
- Make ideas real and test them
- Maintain conceptual integrity through accessibility and implementation quality

### Business goals and desired outcomes

Why organizations invest:

- Grow revenue and create new opportunities
- Improve profitability and operations
- Make stronger investment decisions
- Strengthen competitive advantage
- Get more value from existing technology
- Reduce investment risk
- Improve organizational execution
- Strengthen teams and delivery
- Adapt as needs change

### Problems and unknowns

What buyers may recognize before they know the appropriate engagement:

- Unclear direction or competing priorities
- Operational friction and manual work
- Build, buy, integrate, or modernize decisions
- Underused technology, data, or capabilities
- Product and workflow problems
- Customer or employee experience problems
- Team capability gaps
- AI opportunities without a clear business fit

### Engagements

What a client may purchase. These are distinct from the design approach and contribution modes:

- Opportunity and discovery
- Product direction
- Build vs. buy or modernization decision
- Product design
- Design review and audit
- Product improvement
- Design systems and enablement
- AI opportunity and experience
- Design partnership

### Contribution modes

The role design plays inside an engagement. The source explicitly states that these are not services and are not sequential phases:

- Innovating with design
- Leading with design
- Executing with design
- Supporting with design
- Reviewing with design

An engagement can use multiple contribution modes.

### Capabilities

The expertise used to perform the work:

- Strategy and discovery
- Workshops and facilitation
- Research and validation
- Product and experience design
- Design systems and enablement
- Audits and improvement
- AI and intelligent experiences
- Specialized systems, compliance, writing, and implementation capabilities

### Commercial models

How work can be structured commercially:

- Fixed-fee
- Project-based
- Ongoing partnership
- Capability and enablement

Fractional leadership belongs at this commercial layer. It is not the design model itself and should not become the primary explanation of Dan's everyday work.

## Portfolio implications

The portfolio should make the layers legible in this order:

1. Who Dan is and the outcomes his leadership supports
2. Evidence through selected work and business outcomes
3. His design approach and operating model
4. The different roles he can play within complex work
5. A contained, secondary explanation of selective advisory or fractional availability

The current `Clarity. Strategy. Systems.` language can remain as a concise personal expression of the approach if each part is grounded in the source model:

- Clarity: business goals, desired outcomes, people, activities, constraints, and the real problem
- Strategy: opportunities, priorities, tradeoffs, investment decisions, and a direction teams can act on
- Systems: concepts, workflows, relationships, standards, accessibility, implementation, and conceptual integrity

The portfolio should not use `How I lead`, `How I contribute`, and `Ways to engage` as three parallel descriptions of the same work. The source model gives each phrase a different job:

- How I lead: the design approach and judgment applied throughout the work
- How I contribute: innovating, leading, executing, supporting, or reviewing within an engagement
- Ways to engage: the limited commercial options through which outside organizations can work with Dan
